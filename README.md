## About

I work on the front line of enterprise IT support as a Senior Service Desk Technician at TTEC, resolving escalations and keeping users productive across multiple lines of business in the United States, Mexico, and Europe, including United States public sector accounts.

Outside of work, I build and document labs focused on infrastructure-as-code (Azure Bicep), cloud identity, endpoint hardening, and automation, with the goal of moving into cloud and security engineering.
